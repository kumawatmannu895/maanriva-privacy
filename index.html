Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MAANRIVA ("we", "our", "us" or "the App") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use the MAANRIVA mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Name, mobile number, email address, profile details and authentication-related information.
  • Business information: Business name, category, address, contact details, description and business profile information.
  • Property information: Property or plot details, price, address, property descriptions and related listing information.
  • Photos and videos: Images, videos, reels and other media that you choose to upload.
  • Communication information: Messages and enquiry details exchanged through the App's communication features.
  • Location information: Device location only when you grant the relevant permission and use a location-based feature.
  • Technical and usage information: App version, device or diagnostic information and interactions with app features, where collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Displaying business and property listings.
  • Helping customers discover businesses and properties.
  • Providing chat, call and enquiry-related features.
  • Managing leads, business dashboards and promotional services.
  • Displaying photos, videos and reels uploaded by users.
  • Providing location-related features when permission is granted.
  • Improving app functionality, security and user experience.
  • Responding to support requests and preventing misuse.

3. Public Listings and User Content

Business profiles, property listings, contact details, photos, videos, reels and other content that you choose to publish may be visible to other users or visitors.

Please do not publish passwords, OTPs, financial details or other confidential information in public listings or content. You are responsible for the content you submit.

4. Location and Device Permissions

MAANRIVA accesses device location only when you grant location permission. Location may be used to support location-based property, business or discovery features.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Location-based features may not work when permission is disabled.

Other permissions, such as camera, photos/media or microphone, may be requested when you use a feature that requires them. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

MAANRIVA uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate its services. Depending on the type of information:

  • Render: Used to host and operate backend services.
  • MongoDB: Used to store application data, such as account, business, property and other records handled by the backend.
  • Cloudinary: Used to store and deliver uploaded media such as images and videos.

These providers may process information on our behalf to provide hosting, database, media storage and related services. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide MAANRIVA services.

  • With other users when you choose to publish a listing or communicate with them.
  • With Render, MongoDB and Cloudinary as required to operate the App.
  • With relevant service providers supporting app operations and security.
  • With legal or government authorities when disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain accounts, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the App.

When an account deletion request is completed, we will delete associated account information and user data from systems under our control, including applicable database records and uploaded media, subject to legal requirements and legitimate security or fraud-prevention needs.

Any information that must be retained for legal or legitimate security reasons will be restricted and retained only as necessary for that purpose.

9. Account and Data Deletion

MAANRIVA provides two ways to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Option 1: Delete your account inside the App

Open MAANRIVA, go to your Profile or Account Settings, select the Delete Account option and follow the instructions shown in the App.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the App, send an account deletion request to our support email:

Email: maanriva895@gmail.com

Suggested email subject: MAANRIVA Account Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your registered email address or mobile number.
  • Your MAANRIVA account details sufficient to identify your account.
  • A clear request to delete your account and associated data.

We may verify account ownership before processing the request. Please do not send your password or OTP.

After verification, we will process the deletion request and delete associated data from systems under our control, including applicable MongoDB records and Cloudinary media. Data held by service providers will be addressed as part of the deletion process where applicable.

Certain information may be retained only where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. Account deactivation or temporary suspension alone is not considered account deletion.
